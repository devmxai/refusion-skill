# Changelog

## 0.2.49 (2026-11-20)

- UT-13, a ten-bit export: `export.start` (version 3) takes `ten_bit`, an HEVC Main10 file whose gradients and glows keep no bands of eight bits; `export.presets` (version 5) says whether this device makes one (`ten_bit_available`, and when not, `ten_bit_reason`, the engine's sentence) and whether the choice asked is one (`ten_bit`). Refused with H.264 (`export_ten_bit_needs_hevc`) or where it is not offered (`export_ten_bit_unavailable`, with the reason). An export of a project exported before takes the stretches of the file it made that did not change, already encoded, instead of drawing them again (UT-10): an export with little changed is several times faster.
- WI-2.4 part fourteen, every channel's curve: a channel's `travel` or `settle` may be a ramp of its own numbers, `"travel": "ramp(30%, 70%, 40%)"` (how long the start is left still, how long the end, each 0 to 100 %, and where the peak is, 10 to 90 %); one with a named ramp's numbers is written by its name (`ramp_sharp_start`). `omitted` no longer counts `text_motion_channels` or `edge_channels`.
- WI-2.4 part thirteen, a caption's motion where it is: a sentence or a word has one motion of its own for a phase (`in`, `out`, `loop`) at its level, so `text.motion_set` (version 7) over some sentences takes them from the motion they had and the one set last is drawn; each motion is a run of sentences (or of one sentence's words) that follow each other, written at its first with `"sentences": n` (or `"words": n`), and a run that reaches a sentence or a word giving the same phase is refused (`motion_runs_overlap`): shorten its count or take the other away. A sentence put between a motion's sentences, one moved past another or a word typed between two makes it two runs; a sentence split keeps its motions on both halves. `omitted` no longer counts `caption_motion`.
- WI-2.4 part twelve, what a formula keeps across a cut or a copy: a formula's entry in `formulas` writes `"frame": {"origin": "1.5s", "length": {"reads": "3.8s", "while": "2.3s"}, "noise": "@ghost"}`: what its `time` is at the layer's first instant, the length `layer_duration` reads while the layer is that long (a layer made another length reads its own), and the layer whose noise it has (its key; its number when no layer of the composition has it); each left out where it is the layer's own. A cut, a trim of the front and a copy write it: write it back as read. A formula whose text you change keeps the frame its entry gives. New tool: `property.expression_frame`; `property.expression` reports `frame_origin`, `frame_length_held`, `frame_length_read`, `frame_length_while` and `frame_noise_layer`. `formula_frame` is no longer counted.
- WI-2.4 part eleven: a styled text's `content.text` is always its runs; `text_spans` is no longer counted (it named a text whose content had keys, which a text's content never has).
- WI-2.4 part ten, the rows' names: a row's name is the engine's, given by one rule when the row is made (`V3` for pictures, `A2` for sound, the lowest number free), and no tool names one; the file says a layer's row by its place (`track`). `omitted` no longer counts `track_names`.
- WI-2.4 part nine, a caption's style by name and its roles whole: each of `roles` is a span's style written as a styled part of `content.text` is, by the layer's properties' names: `"roles": {"now": {"fill.color": "#FFD60A", "font.weight": 900, "font.scale": 1.15, "backdrop.color": "#12100E", "backdrop.padding_x": 0.2, "backdrop.padding_y": 0.1, "backdrop.radius": 0.3}}`. The earlier `color` and `pill` are gone: write `fill.color` and `backdrop.color` (with the pill's numbers). `"style": "karaoke"` names a look of `caption.style_catalog`; it is applied first and the caption's own lines laid over it, and the file writes it where the caption is that look exactly. New tool: `caption.role_style_set`, a role's style changed with `text.style_set`'s fields. `caption_roles` is no longer counted.
- WI-2.4 part eight, a text motion's range, wiggly and seed in the file: in a phase of `text_motion` (a text's, a caption's, a sentence's, a word's), `"range": {"start": 0, "end": 50, "offset": 10, "shape": "Ramp up", "pass": "Forward"}` (which units take how much of the motion, in percent of the units), `"wiggly": {"amount": 60, "rate": "Medium"}` (how unevenly in time) and `"seed": 3` (which draw a random order, a noise or a wiggly is); `null` takes a range or a wiggly away, and one you leave out keeps the phase's own. `text.motion_set` (version 6) takes `seed`. None of the three is counted under `omitted` any more.
- WI-2.4 part seven, formulas in the file: a layer's `formulas`, by property: `"formulas": {"transform.rotation": "time / 1s * 90deg", "transform.position": "layer(\"@title\").transform.position + [0, 120px]"}`, in the language of `property.expression_set`. Another layer of the same composition is named by its key, `layer("@key")` and `measure("@key")`; the engine keeps its number. A formula whose output was put by hand (a drag on the canvas) is `{"formula": "...", "adjust": [24, -8]}`, the adjustment in the property's rule: a difference for a position, a turn and most numbers, a ratio for a scale and an opacity (`1.2`). `null` takes a formula away, `"formulas": null` every one; a formula you leave out of `formulas` stays. New tool: `property.expression_adjust` (the adjustment by numbers, or none). Formulas are no longer counted under `omitted`; what a formula keeps across a cut or a copy is (`formula_frame`).
- WI-2.4 part six (b), a caption's motion in the file: a caption layer's `text_motion` is its motion over every sentence, as a text's (its whole is `"unit": "Sentence"`): `"text_motion": {"in": {"preset": "rise", "unit": "Word", "duration": "0.35s", "stagger": "0.06s"}}`. A motion over some sentences is in the first one's entry in `sentences`, with `"sentences": 2` in the phase for it and the next; over some words of one sentence, in the first word's entry, with `"words": 2`. The narrower wins where they meet. A sentence's motion you leave out is none; a word's is kept where its sentence gives no `words`. Every motion tool takes the caption's scope by name, `caption_scope` (1 every sentence, 2 sentences `caption_first`..`caption_last`, 3 words `caption_first`..`caption_last` of sentence `caption_cue`; 0, the owner's choice, as before): `text.motion_set` (version 5), `text.motion_clear` (3), `text.motion` (4), `text.motion_copy` (3), `text.motion_paste` (3), `motion.channels` (3), `motion.channel_set` (3) and `inspector.add` (3). A recipe over some sentences is applied over them; a role is not a caption's. Taking a sentence or a word away (in the file or in the app) takes the motions over it with it. A motion over sentences that are not next to each other, or one a project saved before still holds over a sentence it no longer has, is counted (`caption_motion`).
- WI-2.4 part six (a), a caption's words in the file: a caption layer writes `"captions": {"language": "ar", "times": "layer", "sentences": [{"from": "0.6s", "to": "1.6s", "text": "برغر سماش", "words": [{"from": "0.6s", "to": "1.05s"}, {"from": "1.05s", "to": "1.6s", "key": true}]}]}` (`times` is `"source"` for a caption bound to a video's time, with its `source`; `speaker` on a sentence), and `"roles": {"now": {"color": "#FF4D1A"}, "spoken": {"color": "#F7EFE4"}, "upcoming": {"color": "#F7EFE400"}}`, each with a `pill`. Write a sentence, a word's time, a key word or a role's colour and it is set; a word retimed is timed by hand. A word's `timing`, `confidence` and `marked` are the engine's account: write them back as read, or leave them out. A caption's times are written exactly, never on the frame grid. A caption you make with no anchor stands with its box's foot middle at its place (with no place, at the canvas's foot, in the middle); give it its `font.*` and `paragraph.align`, or it takes a fresh text's look. `omitted` no longer counts `caption_words`; a caption's motion over its sentences comes next.
- The defects of a new project built live in its file (DF-61 to DF-68): a transition is no longer taken away by a save that makes another layer, and one that is taken away is said in the reply; a value is kept as you write it (0.95 stays 0.95), and one outside its range is refused with the range; a layout taken away and a position given in one save both apply; a layer's first frame with motion blur no longer shows it whole before it comes in; a group's or a scene layer's ready motion (a pop, an emphasis) grows from its middle; a pop made by the role `enter` bounces (the fast spatial spring) and is opaque at once; a pin and a layout measure a layer at its set scale (an icon scaled up takes its room) and a group by its content, not its shadow. A group's own effect is still cut at its canvas: give a shadowed layer room inside its group.
- WI-2.4 part five, the project's mode and colour mix in the head: `"project_mode": "composition"` is written for a project whose length is its own (left out for a sequence, every project made now); it is set when the project is made, so a file opened as a new project makes it, and another is refused (`header_differs`). `"color_mix": "display"` or `"linear"` is the space the keys of colours are mixed in (left out for `perceptual`, OKLab, the default): `scene.write` sets it (`project.set_color_mix`); the project file refuses another (`needs_owner`), as it does the motion blur and the motion system, and the owner's is written into it after `fps`. A file that has a line refused by the file's own check gets `scene.write`'s refusals in the same reply. A line the engine takes away from your file takes only its own line: every comment stays. `rpro document check` makes a composition's file as one. `omitted` no longer counts `color_mix` or `project_mode`.
- WI-2.4 part four, a layer's name: `"name"` on a layer now names it (it was only remarked on); a layer the file makes with no name is named by its key, and the file writes a name only where it is not the key. The tool is `layer.rename`. In the app, the selected layer's name stands at the head of its properties panel, and a touch on it changes it. `scene.write`'s and `scene.document`'s own descriptions now say what they make and what they still count.
- WI-2.4 part three, markers and the beat in the file: `"markers": [{"at": "2s", "name": "Drop", "for": "0.5s", "color": "Orange"}]` in the head (the project's), on a scene's entry and on a layer (its time from the layer's `at`, and it goes with the layer); `for` left out for a point, `color` left out for red. `"beat": {"bpm": 120, "phase": "0s", "bar": 3}` on the head or a scene (`bar` left out for 4; `"-0.5s"` for a beat 0 before the start; `null` takes it away). Markers are paired with the ones there by their order in time; written back as they read, nothing changes. Neither is counted under `omitted` any more.
- WI-2.4 part two: `omitted` no longer counts what is written. A sound's volume and fades are written (`audio.volume`, `audio.fade_in`, `audio.fade_out`) and were also counted under `sound`; a row of sound (`A2`) is named by the engine and was counted under `track_names`. The owner's ad counted 132 things; it now counts 2 (a caption's words, a text animator).
- WI-2.4 part one, a text styled in parts: `content.text` may be a list of runs, a string for a plain part and an object for a styled one, by the layer's own properties' names: `"content.text": ["Fire ", {"text": "noodles", "fill.color": "#FF5A2C", "font.weight": 800}, " tonight"]`. A run sets `font.family`, `font.weight`, `font.style` (`Normal`, `Italic`), `fill.color`, `font.scale` (a multiple of `font.size`), `font.tracking`, `font.word_spacing`, and a pill behind it (`backdrop.color` with `backdrop.padding_x`, `backdrop.padding_y`, `backdrop.radius`). `paragraphs` gives each line its own layout, one entry per line, `null` for a plain one: `paragraph.direction` (`Auto`, `LTR`, `RTL`), `paragraph.indent`, `paragraph.spacing`. The file writes a text as runs only where a part is styled; a text read back as it was written changes nothing. A text's parts and paragraphs are no longer counted under `omitted`.
- WI-2.3 part two, an address of the internet: where the file names a file it may give an `https://` address. `{"key": "logo", "kind": "shape", "fill_source": "https://…/logo.png", "at": "0s", "for": "3s", "shape.size": [800, 600]}` paints a shape with the picture, laid to cover it; `{"kind": "audio", "source": "https://…"}` makes a sound and `{"kind": "video", "source": "https://…"}` a video (on the project's own timeline). The engine fetches it into the project (a project folder's `downloads` folder) under `asset.fetch`'s rules; the layer is made at once and stands as a declared placeholder (grey, silent, unavailable) until it is in, and a clip with no `for` stands 5 s until its source gives its length, which the engine then writes back. The reply's `assets` says `downloading` (a plan: `to_download`), then is amended `ready`, or `failed` with why. An address that will never be fetched (not https, a private host) is refused at its line; the same address again uses the same download. The file writes a fetched file back by its address. `asset.fetch` now works in a project folder too (`downloads`). A sequence's `for` left as it was no longer refuses a save whose layers make it longer. Not yet: a drawing by address, an address into a shape that is there, a video by address in a scene.
- WI-2.3 part one, assets by name: `source` names what a layer shows, a file of the library by its name, else an asset of the built-in pack by its id. `{"key": "like", "kind": "vector", "source": "icon.heart", "at": "0s", "for": "2s"}` makes an icon; `{"key": "whoosh_1", "kind": "audio", "source": "sound.whoosh", "at": "40f"}` a sound of its own length (`for` trims it). Both work inside a scene, and in a project folder (the sound is kept in its `sounds` folder; it was refused there, `no_library_project_open`). The file writes the pack's id back, and the library's name for the same sound reads as no change. The reply lists each asset brought (`assets`). `asset.insert` given a scene as its target now puts the icon or the sound in that scene (it went to the project's own timeline). A name the library lacks is refused; an address of the internet and a font the device lacks come with parts two and three.
- WI-2.2, transitions in the file: `"transition": {"kind": "motion.whip", "for": "16f", "direction": "right"}` on the layer that goes out of a cut puts a transition between it and the layer that begins where it ends on its row; no id, no second pass. `align` is `center` (left out), `start` or `end`, or `"lead": "4f"` for its time before the cut; `curve` a curve's name (`smooth`) or `"ramp"` with `ramp_out`, `ramp_in`, `ramp_peak`; each setting by its key in `transition.catalog`, a choice by its key. A setting or curve left out is the kind's own; `policy` is `hold` when left out. `null` takes it away. Two layers on two rows are refused `not_same_row`, naming the other layer: give them one row with `track`. Transitions no longer count under `omitted`.
- WI-2.1, a grade's look in the file: `"grade.look": "cinematic.golden_hour"` lays a look over the grade of a shape (a photo shown in one among them), a video or an adjustment layer, by its id as `grade.looks` lists it (one of your own looks too, `own.…`); `null` takes it away and the grade's own numbers stay. How much of it shows is `grade.look_amount` (0 to 100), a value or a motion: `{"keys": [["0s", 0], ["1.5s", 100]]}` brings it in. A look's name is refused with its id to write. A grade switched off reads `"grade.enabled": false`; no tool switches it yet, so another value is refused (`grade_switch_unsupported`).
- WI-2.1, a gradient's stops in the file: `"fill.stops": [["0%", "#FF5A2C"], ["50%", "#FFD23F"], ["100%", "#7B5CFF"]]` on a layer whose fill is a gradient (`"fill.kind": "Linear gradient"`, a radial or a conic one, or an evolving field's ramp), and `"stroke_paint.stops"` for a shape's stroke. Each stop is its place, a percent, and its colour; either may move: `["50%", {"keys": [["0s", "#FFD23F"], ["2s", "#7C5CFF"]]}]`, and a moving place is in percents too, `[{"keys": [["0s", "20%"], ["2s", "80%"]]}, "#FFD23F"]`. Matched by order: more entries add stops at the end, fewer take the last ones away, two at least (`stops_too_few`). A gradient is made whole in one save, its kind, line and stops together. A colour's fill has no stops (`fill_not_gradient`). Stops no longer count under `omitted`.
- WI-2.9, a camera from the file: `"kind": "camera"` is made from the file, at the middle of its composition's canvas (its anchor is its frame's middle). Its frame stays on the canvas, so a camera moves once zoomed in (`transform.scale` below 100): pan it by keying `transform.position`. A document holding a camera now writes back with no change (its zoom is the engine's).
- WI-3.7, measuring a text (DF-34): `text.layout_snapshot` (version 4) takes `text`, other words measured in the layer's style (how wide, how many lines; nothing is changed), and `carets` (`"3 7 12"`), where a caret stands after that many characters, in one reading: no probe layers for a typed line's partial texts.
- WI-3.7, a hand's drag written as the layout: in the app, an item of a layout dragged on the canvas takes its place in the order (written in the layout's `items`), and a pinned layer dragged moves its pin's offsets; the file shows the change. Fixed with it: an edit made with a scene open no longer takes the main timeline's values out of `project.rfp` (DF-59).
- WI-3.7, a layout's change eased: `"moves": {"for": "0.4s", "ease": "out_cubic"}` on a layout lays out only the items shown, and when one comes or goes the others ease to their new places along the curve. A chat that grows upward is a column with `"justify": "end"` that moves: each new message pushes the ones before it up. `composition.set_layout` takes `moves_for` and `moves_ease`.
- WI-3.7, a grid: `"layout": {"stack": "grid", "columns": 3, "gap": 24}` stands a scene's items in cells, row by row; the columns share the width equally (in a group, each as wide as the widest item) and a row is as tall as its tallest item. `align` places each item down its cell and `justify` across it; `"size": ["fill", "hug"]` takes the cell's width. Change `columns` and the whole grid stands again, with no position written. `composition.set_layout` takes `columns` (version 2).
- WI-3.7, a layer's size: `"size": ["hug", "fill"]`, across then down. `hug` is its own size (a text's words, a shape's `shape.size`, a group's box); `fill` takes the room its layout gives it (a bar as wide as its column). `"size_max": [960, null]` wraps a longer text at 960 and lets it grow down, so the layers after it in a column move; `"size_min"` keeps it at least that large. A chat bubble or a pill is one text with its backdrop, as large as its words, with no place or size written. A text's box that a pin or a layout measures is now the one you see, its lines' full height: an Arabic pill in a group is no longer cut. The tool is `layer.set_size`.
- WI-3.8, groups: `{"key": "comment", "kind": "group", "at": "16s", "for": "2s", "layers": [ ... ]}` holds its layers inside it, each placed from the group's top left and timed from its start. It moves, fades and keeps its order as one: its opacity is over the group's picture, and its box is the engine's, as far as its layers reach. A group takes a `pin` like any layer with a box. Its layers are addressed `@comment/panel` in replies and errors; a save that changes one of them changes that one alone. A group is made in one save; duplicating its layer copies it. The tool marking a scene as a group's own is `composition.set_group`.
- WI-3.7, a scene's own layout: `"layout": {"stack": "row", "gap": 20, "align": "center", "justify": "center"}` on a scene stands its layers in a row (or a `column`) at their own sizes, with no position written; a longer text or a larger box moves the ones after it. `items` lists the layers it places, in order; a layer left out, or pinned, stays free. The tool is `composition.set_layout`.
- WI-3.7, pins: `"pin": {"center": "@badge"}` centres a label in its pill, `{"top": "@card.bottom + 16", "left": "@card"}` puts it under a card, `{"right": 48, "bottom": 48}` keeps it in a corner of its container. One key across and one down; a number is the distance inward from the container (its parent's box, or the canvas). The engine places the box at every instant, so it follows a text's length or a card that moves; write no `transform.position` for a layer pinned on both axes. The tool is `layer.set_pin`.
- WI-2.8, templates and slots in the project's file:
  - A scene's `"template": {"controls": [{"id": "dish", "kind": "text", "layer": "dish_name"}], "intro": "0.4s", "outro": "0.3s", "hold": "loop"}`: its controls in the inspector's order, each by its id, kind, layer, name (made from the id when left out) and, for a colour or a number, its property; `null` makes it a plain scene.

  - A shape's `"slot": "Dish photo"` (or an object with its description, what it takes, its proportions, focus and fit); `"fill_source": null` empties a filled slot.
  - A control changed in place keeps every copy's values; a template's pictures keep their files when the project opens again; a refusal in a scene file's own field or a list's item points at its line.
  - A layer that shows a scene is placed by the scene's top-left corner.
  - A shape with `fill_source` naming a picture is made inside a scene too (it was refused there, `create_not_yet`).

- The design table (WI-2.6): `project.design_table` reads how the open project's files are built, as numbers: hand numbers and place fields a layer, raw shapes, layers grouped under nulls, hand keys by property, the share of moving layers with ready motion, layers placed by a word. `rpro design-stats <folder>...` gives the same table from the command line, and each save writes its file's table into `.refusion/metrics.json`. An effect's region is its entry's `"region": {"masks": [0], "inverted": true}`, naming the layer's masks by their places in `masks` (a mask the same save makes has no id yet). - A layer's track matte is in the project's file (WI-2.1, second part): `"matte": {"from": "shape_key", "mode": "Luma", "hide_source": false}` on the layer it cuts, `from` named as `parent` is (in the same composition), `mode` `Alpha` and `hide_source` true when left out, `null` to take it away. `matte` no longer counts under `omitted`.
- A layer's effects are in the project's file (WI-2.1, first part): `"fx": [{"kind": "stylize.glow", "threshold": 15, "radius": 70}]`, in the order they are drawn, each kind by its name in `effect.catalog`, each setting by its key there (a value or a motion), a choice by its variant's name, `"enabled": false` to switch one off.
