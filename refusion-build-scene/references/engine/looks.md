## Liquid

### Liquid wobble — `fx.look:liquid_wobble`

Liquid · suits video, shape, vector, text, background, scene, adjustment · cost unmeasured · available

A ready-made chain of 2 effects, added in one step: Turbulent Displace, Wave Warp.

**Use:** A picture or a title that should look underwater, melted or seen through heat: a dream, a flashback, a playful wobble. The warp moves by itself all the while the layer is there, so it needs no keyframes; lower the amount for a gentle shimmer.
